# Operator Outreach — recruit 3–5 for the concierge validation (doc 10)

**Goal:** get 3–5 real operators to try the free 2-week concierge so you validate demand while Fable builds.

## Who to target (best fit)
- On **Guesty or Hostaway**, **10–100 listings**, listed on **multiple channels** (Airbnb + Booking.com at least), and **manual-first** (they set promos by hand, no RMS). Your dconsult network + STR Facebook/WhatsApp groups + r/AirBnBHosts.

Get: which channel manager + channels they use, how they set promos today (and how much time it takes), their biggest revenue frustration, and read access to (or a screen-share of) their calendar/rates/promotions. Pick one treated + one control listing (doc 10).
